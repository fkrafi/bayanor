## Our Story

BAYANOR was founded with a singular vision: to elevate the standard of global trade from the United Arab Emirates. Headquartered in Dubai — the world's most strategically positioned trade hub — we bridge premium global manufacturers with regional and international buyers through a trusted, streamlined distribution network.

Our name, **BAYANOR**, is a reflection of our core values: clarity (*bayan*) and light (*nor*) — guiding principles that shape every business relationship we build.

## What We Do

We operate as a full-service B2B trading house, offering:

- Strategic product sourcing from verified global suppliers
- End-to-end import and export logistics management
- B2B distribution to regional and international retailers
- Private label and custom procurement solutions
- Quality assurance and compliance management

## Our Business Activities

Our licensed activity portfolio is curated to serve high-demand commercial markets:

- **E-Commerce** — Online buying and selling operations for digital-first commerce
- **E-Commerce Through Social Media** — Commercial social account operations under existing licenses
- **General Trading** — Importing, trading, and re-exporting approved goods
- **General Trading Import & Export** — Broad goods trading excluding restricted categories
- **Human Resources Consultancy** — Workforce planning, advisory, and performance programs
- **Events Organizing & Managing** — End-to-end planning and operational event delivery
- **Software Development Services** — Custom software and mobile application solutions
- **Innovation Startup** — Development and launch of novel products and technologies
- **Branding Services** — Brand strategy, visual identity, and corporate positioning
- **Foodstuff Trading Import & Export** — Wholesale trade of canned and preserved food products
